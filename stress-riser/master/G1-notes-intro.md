## G1. How to read the raw research notes

The chapters G3 to G10 are the **working notes** the eight research agents saved while they worked, copied unchanged. They are the most detailed record: every URL, quotation, number and grade, including dead ends and corrections. They are not edited for reading. Please note:

- Long scratch-folder paths (`/tmp/…/scratchpad/research/…`) were shortened to `research/…` in this file; nothing else was changed.
- Some lines mention scratch folders (for example `thumbs/` or `raw/`) and helper scripts. Those folders held downloaded pages and images that are not part of this repository; the conclusions that matter are in the final reports (Part D).
- The notes were written before the effort cap and before the independent reviews. Where the reviewers later corrected a claim, the correction is in Part E and in the corrected report (Part C), not in the notes.
- Evidence grades: **[A]** official documentation, peer-reviewed or a large dataset; **[B]** first-hand data from a creator or company, or a correlational dataset; **[C]** opinion, vendor blog or inference.
- Chapter order: G3 platform; G4 CTR evidence; G5 psychology; G6 niche audit; G7 cartoon audit; G8 story visuals (the fact base for the examples); G9 design system; G10 packaging.
- Chapter G2 (before them) holds the exact instructions each agent received.
