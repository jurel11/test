## B6. Review audit trail, process log and spend

### B6.1 Why a review cycle

The channel's rule is "nothing invented, ever". The first complete draft (the report, ten mockups and the gallery) was therefore sent to two independent reviewers who had not written it: one checked every claim against sources, the other checked the brief, the palette, the character rules and the design. Their full reports are in Part E. The findings below are summarised with their resolution. Statuses: **Fixed**, **Partly** (changed but a residual issue remains), **Open** (needs the owner's decision), **Moot** (the item no longer applies after a redesign).
