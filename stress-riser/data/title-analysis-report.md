# Title analysis of the two video indexes (generated)

Generated by `tools/title_analysis.py` from `data/niche-video-index.csv` and `data/cartoon-video-index.csv` (fetched 2026-09-30). Views are the abbreviated figures YouTube lists (for example 11M, 172K), so they are coarse; they also depend on age. The two groups are not random samples: the top group is each channel's most-viewed list, the weak group its weakest recent uploads. Everything below is correlation inside that sample, not proof that a title feature causes views. The z-score gap is the difference in mean within-channel z of log10(views) between titles with and without the feature (0.5 is half a standard deviation of that channel's own spread); only channels with at least four titles enter it.
