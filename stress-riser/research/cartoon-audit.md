## Own-data findings (grade B: first-hand platform data + my visual coding; n small)
1. Ink Explainer (the brief's reference): top 6 by views all have (a) 2-3 words of thumbnail text in all-caps, yellow (or white) with thick black outline, top third of frame; (b) a full illustrated scene (cave, savanna, desert, snow) behind; (c) 1-3 stick figures, round white head, circle eyes with lids, single-line limbs, fur costume as the only prop; (d) text that answers/teases the question title rather than repeating it (title "What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Day?" -> "NO JOBS"; 9.7M). Lowest 3 of 16: white-background IKEA-effect explainer (15K), dark skeleton cave "I WILL DIE" (37K), crowded battle "FIRST GUN?" (42K). Topic confound: ancient-humans questions vs off-format topics.
2. Clone family (Explain In Paint, Zenn, Axen, HUMAN-ISH, Hypothetically-like) shows a NATURAL EXPERIMENT: near-identical templates give 548K on one video and 4-12K on the next on the same channel (HUMAN-ISH). Template is necessary-ish, not sufficient; topic, title and age dominate. Low-level pixel features (mean luminance, saturation, edge density as clutter proxy, yellow share, white share, black share) did NOT separate top from weak within channels (44 channels, top>weak in 17-29 of 44 for every metric, i.e. coin-flip). Age/era confounds all top-vs-weak comparisons (top = all-time, weak = recent).
3. Phone-size test (176 px wide, light + dark feed): white/pale-background thumbnails (Ink Explainer IKEA, Zenn ARE WE ALIENS?, MinuteEarth, Casually Explained, Sam O'Nella) lose their edge on the white feed (light mode) and pop only in dark mode; full-scene saturated thumbnails (NO JOBS, COZY INSIDE?, FREE ALL DAY, PREGNANT?) keep a boundary in both. 1-3 word 30%-height text stays legible; small labels, "before/after" captions, and multi-line text do not. Small stick figures (<12% of frame height) read only by posture, not face.
4. Recurrent cross-channel formulas: see final report.
5. Gore/shock cartoon channels (Serious History, Chat History, Bernard, StickTory, Hypothetically) get 1-7M with a big single crying/screaming face + arrow + 2-4 words, but they conflict with the brief (no gore, no sexualization) - use the layout, not the content.

## Caveats
- View counts are rounded strings; ages differ from 3 days to 13 years; "weak" = relative to same channel and partly reflects age and topic.
- No CTR/impression data for any channel (only owners have it). All "what works" statements are correlational patterns in view counts + visual coding, not causal.
- Thumbnail text in many cases is composited over AI-looking art; the brief forbids text/arrows inside scene images, so overlays must be added in a separate compositing step (check with user).
