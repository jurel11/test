## 1. 1of10 Media "What Actually Makes a YouTube Video Go Viral" [B, large dataset, correlational, NOT peer reviewed]
URL: https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/ (published 2026-02-06, modified 2026-08-15; fetched raw via curl 2026-09-30)
- Dataset: 300,000+ high-performing videos published 2025, 62.6B views, 52,000 channels. Metric = Outlier Score = video views / channel median views. (Only "viral"/high performing videos -> selection on outcome; measures VIEWS not CTR.)
- Faces: face vs no-face similar overall; multiple faces > single face; faces only meaningfully help channels 200K+ subs. Faces help: Lifestyle/Vlogs, Finance, Beauty. Hurt: Health&Fitness, Movies&TV. "Faces amplify existing interest, they don't create it."
- Text: 84% of thumbnails have text; thumbnails with text get ~19% fewer views. Best: no text, or <10 characters covering <7% of image.
- Color: best dominant colors cyan, green, yellow/orange. Cyan ~36% more views than average. Brightness: views increase with brightness; median peak at brightness score 100-110 (scale not defined; likely 0-255 mean luminance - UNVERIFIED units). Dark thumbnails underperform.
- Titles: 5 words highest median; >6 words decline; 10+ underperform; <=30 chars ~60% more views; numbers in title ~11% FEWER views; negative titles (11% of sample) ~22% more views than positive; very high readability ~20% more.
- Entertainment vs Education: entertainment ~44% more median views than educational. Education "must borrow entertainment framing, use emotional hooks, avoid lecture-style".
- Niche macro-group: "Politics / History" listed under "Blockbuster niches: high upside, high variance".
- Video length peak 18-24 min (not relevant to this slice).
- Also SEJ (2025-12-22) https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/ reports the same, quotes Nate Curtiss (1of10 Head of Content) pushing back on "remove your face" claim.

## 2. YouTube official Test & Compare [A official documentation]
URL https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en
- Up to 3 titles and/or thumbnails; winner decided by WATCH TIME SHARE (not CTR): Winner / Performed Same / Inconclusive; if no clear winner first uploaded shown. Tests should complete within 2 weeks; desktop-only setup; cannot test Shorts (as of doc fetch), premieres etc. Small % of traffic held as control.
- Rene Ritchie (YouTube Creator Liaison) quote via SEJ: "Thumbnail Test & Compare returns watch time rather than separate metrics on click-through rate (CTR) and retention (AVP), because watch time includes both! You have to click to watch and you have to retain to build up time. If you over-index on CTR, it could become click-bait, which could tank retention, and hurt performance. This way, the tool helps build good habits — thumbnails that make a promise and videos that deliver on it!" [A/B: official YouTube employee statement]

## 5. Netflix artwork tests [B, company first-hand; movie/TV art, not YouTube]
URL (official, fetched raw): http://about.netflix.com/en/news/the-power-of-a-picture (Nick Nelson, ~2016) + tech blog by Gopal Krishnan https://netflixtechblog.com/selecting-the-best-artwork-for-videos-through-a-b-testing-f6155c4595f6 (Medium/Cloudflare blocked; content via secondary)
- Early-2014 consumer research: artwork = biggest influencer on decision to watch, "over 82% of their focus while browsing"; users spent avg 1.8 seconds considering each title.
- "faces with complex emotions outperform stoic or benign expressions"
- "using visible, recognizable characters (and especially polarizing ones) results in more engagement"; villains outperformed heroes in kids & action.
- "an image's tendency to win dramatically dropped when it contained more than 3 people" -> too complex at small sizes/smaller screens.
- "better images ... significantly increased overall streaming hours" (no numbers).
- The oft-quoted "brain processes images in 13 ms" appears here as background claim, NOT a thumbnail-click finding -> the 13 ms is from lab picture-detection research (Potter et al. 2014, MIT) - see myths.
- Commenter caveat: villain images may win because of contrast/face clarity (confounded).

## 6. Academic
- Koh & Cui 2022, Decision Support Systems vol.160 (Sept 2022) DOI 10.1016/j.dss.2022.113820 [A peer-reviewed]: 3,745 marketing videos from 38 top brands, 4 industries (auto, beverage, restaurant, toy); visual attributes of thumbnails significantly related to view-through; high colorfulness+high brightness OR low colorfulness+low brightness (i.e., coherent) with moderate image quality are more effective; brightness, celebrity presence, colorfulness = peripheral cues; complexity/object count = central route. Caveat: branded content, view-through not CTR. (abstract via secondary search summary; publisher paywalled)
- Fang, Zheng, Liao, Han, Liu 2026, J. Academy of Marketing Science 54:815-837, DOI 10.1007/s11747-026-01152-6 [A]: 22,958 UGV thumbnails + two randomized experiments; INVERTED-U between visual complexity and popularity; U-shaped for visual coherence; stronger for utilitarian videos & highly typical videos. Abstract via https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/joamsc/v54y2026i3d10.1007_s11747-026-01152-6.html (raw fetch OK). Platform: UGV platform (not verified which -- UNVERIFIED whether YouTube).
- Cui, Chung, Peng, Wang 2024, J. Business Research 183:114849 [A] "Clicks for money": 206,221 logs, 323 thumbnails+titles (which platform: see search; abstract says GLS regression); strong sentiment in thumbnails boosts views; captions w/ strong sentiment reduce views; positive titles more views; punctuation & emoticons increase clicks. (secondary summary; UNVERIFIED platform)
- Face presence in UGV (Int. J. Research in Marketing? sciencedirect S0167811625000096, 2025) [A]: 4 studies (large data, 2 experiments, eye tracking): moderate face presence 30-40% of frames yields highest engagement; effect weaker for influencers >160K followers. (video content, not thumbnails; secondary summary)
- "A Study on Video Thumbnails Design Attributes and Their Influence" (2024, RED platform, n=30 videos) [C - tiny sample]: far background +36.49% views vs close; group thumbnails -37.4% views vs single person; two-line titles +44.2% likes. Contradicts 1of10 multi-face finding.
- arXiv 2607.23739 (26 Jul 2026, Naveed et al.) misleading thumbnails detection: cites "Srinivasan et al. 2021" for claim misleading thumbnails boost CTR by 14% (secondhand; original not verified).

## 7. CTR benchmarks — official
- YouTube Help "Impressions & click-through-rate FAQs" https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154?hl=en (fetched raw 2026-09-30) [A official]: "Half of all channels and videos on YouTube have an impressions CTR that can range between 2% and 10%." New videos/channels (<1 week) or <100 views see wider range. "If a video gets a lot of impressions (such as if it appears on the Home Page), it's natural for the CTR to be lower. Videos where most impressions are from sources like your channel page may have a higher rate." Warns: avoid checking CTR right after upload; small changes normal; "Testing several thumbnails or titles on the same video [manually]. It's difficult to make sure each video is being seen by the same audience. Differences in CTR might be due to traffic sources". "Avoid trying to increase CTR using clickbait... You can tell if your thumbnail is clickbait if it's getting high CTR but low average view duration and lower than expected impressions." Clickbait videos have low AVD -> less likely to be recommended.
- Todd Beaupré (YouTube Sr Director Growth & Discovery) in Creator Insider interview 2026-09-01 "YouTube Algorithm 2026 - What Creators Need to Know" (via https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/ ) [A-/B official, secondhand write-up, note ppc.land critiques: no sample/methodology]: "probably all your videos have about maybe a 10% or less click-through rate" in the feed of subscribers; "No metric on its own is a good indicator of value"; CTR "falls predictably as impressions widen beyond core fans"; ranking weights vary by person/device/moment; mobile thumbnails used to be much smaller; "Often less is more" (density/choice); early performance learned separately per audience cohort (subscribers vs non).
- SEJ 2025 article on Beaupré/Ritchie interview: satisfaction (surveys) counts, not only CTR/watch time. https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-youtubes-recommendation-system-works-in-2025/538379/
- YouTube extended A/B (Test & Compare) from thumbnails to titles, Dec 9 2025 (per ppc.land timeline; Rene Ritchie LinkedIn Oct 2025 also announced) - winner = watch time per impression.
- SEO-blog benchmark tables by traffic source (Search 12.5%, Suggested 9.5%, Browse 3.5%, External 2.8%; Education 4.5%, Gaming 8.5%) from focus-digital.co etc. = NO verifiable methodology -> treat [C]/UNVERIFIED. (URL: https://focus-digital.co/average-youtube-ctr-organic-paid-benchmarks-2026/)

## 8. Google/YouTube research on CTR vs watch time [A peer-reviewed + official]
- Covington, Adams, Sargin (Google), "Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations", RecSys 2016. PDF https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//pubs/archive/45530.pdf (downloaded, text extracted): "Our final ranking objective is constantly being tuned based on live A/B testing results but is generally a simple function of expected watch time per impression. Ranking by click-through rate often promotes deceptive videos that the user does not complete ('clickbait') whereas watch time better captures engagement." [A]
- YouTube Help CTR FAQ (see 7): high CTR + low AVD + lower-than-expected impressions = clickbait signature.
- YouTube Help "Check your YouTube thumbnail impressions": an impression is counted if thumbnail shown >1 second and >=50% visible on screen; CTR = "How often viewers watched a video after seeing a thumbnail". https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7577430
- Shorts: ppc.land (2026-07/08) reports YouTube began rolling out custom thumbnails for Shorts on 2026-07-25 (Partner Program, desktop, no A/B), and that YouTube's Shorts product lead put share of Shorts views from the Shorts feed at 99.9% (Jan 2025) -> Shorts thumbnail matters mostly for channel page/search, not feed. https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/ [B secondhand news; original Creator Insider not fetched]

## 10. vidIQ 2026 thumbnail study [B, company data; NO control group]
URL https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/ (Vercel checkpoint blocked raw fetch; numbers via search summary + frameos.studio critique https://frameos.studio/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices)
- 500 breakout long-form videos, 30 niches, published in the month ending 2026-06-30; filters: >=2 min, channels >=10K subs, >=50K views; "breakout" = outperforming own channel (vidIQ Outliers).
- Face in 69% of 500 (75% of top 100, 80% of top 50 overperformers); high contrast 56%; face OR high contrast 89%; overlay text 72% (median 5 words among those with text); exaggerated expression only ~5%.
